Ingredients:

  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter
  • 6 ounces bittersweet chockolate
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 large egg yolks
  • 1/4 cup gradulated sugar
  • 1/8 teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons all purpose flour

    Instructions:

  1. preheat the oven to 450F
  2. Butter and lightly flour 6 4-5oz ramekins and place them on a baking sheet
  3. Melt the butter and chocolate in a microwave safe bowl stirring every 30-seconds until melted. Whisk until smooth.
  4. In a large bowl, combine the eggs, egg yolks, sugar, and salt. Beat on medium speed until thick and pale yellow. Fold in chocolate mixture and flour until well combined.
  5. Divide the batter among the prepared ramekins
  6. Bake for 6-8 minutes or until the sides of the cakes are firm set but the center is jiggly. Let the cake cool in the ramekin for 1 minute.
  7. Place a small plate over the top of each ramekin and carefully turn it over, inverting the cake onto the plate.
  8. Let stand for about 10 seconds, then remove the ramekin to unmold the cakes. Sift powdered sugar on top and garnish with berries if desired. Serve immediately.
