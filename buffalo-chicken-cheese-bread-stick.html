Preheat the oven to 350°F. Slice the Frenchloaf in half and hollow out the center. Make the garlic butter. In a bowl, mix the softened butter, roasted garlic, olive oil, Parmesan, red pepper flakes, and black pepper until smooth. Spread the mixture evenly over both halves of the bread. Bake for 20 minutes until the bread is lightly toasted and fragrant. Remove it from the oven. Make the buffalo chicken filling. In a bowl, combine the shredded chicken, buffalo sauce, cheddar cheese, green onions, and cream cheese. Mix until everything is fully coated and creamy. Increase the oven temperature to 375°F. Pack the buffalo chicken mixture tightly onto the garlic bread. Top with mozzarella and cheddar, covering the chicken mixture completely. Bake for another 20 minutes, until the cheese on top is melted and golden, and the inside is hot and bubbly. Finish with chopped parsley. Slice the bread and serve warm.

    Garlic Butter: 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter, softened 1 head roasted garlic 1 tablespoon olive oil ¼ cup grated Parmesan 1 teaspoon red pepper flakes ½ teaspoon black pepper
    Buffalo Chicken Filling: 1 rotisserie chicken, shredded (about 3 cups) 1 cup Frank’s RedHot 8 oz cheddar cheese, shredded ⅓ cup green onions, chopped ¼ cup whipped cream cheese
    Topping: 1 cup shredded mozzarella 1 cup shredded cheddar Chopped parsley, for finishing
