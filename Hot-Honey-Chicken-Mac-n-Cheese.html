Hot Honey Chicken Mac & Cheese

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • Oil for cooking

Mac & Cheese

  • 8 oz elbow macaroni
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 2 cups milk
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Hot Honey

  • 1/2 cup honey
  • 1/2 tbsp hot sauce
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1 tbsp butter

Instructions

  1. Cook the macaroni according to the package directions. Drain and set aside.
  2. Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces and season the flour.
  3. Mix the egg and milk together. Dip the chicken in the mixture, then coat it with flour.
  4. Cook the chicken in oil until golden brown and fully cooked.
  5. Melt butter in a pot and stir in the flour.
  6. Slowly add the milk and stir until the sauce thickens.
  7. Add the cheddar and mozzarella cheese and stir until melted.
  8. Add the macaroni to the cheese sauce and mix together.
  9. Warm the honey, hot sauce, red pepper flakes, and butter in a small pan.
  10. Put the mac and cheese in a bowl, add the chicken, and drizzle the hot honey on top.
